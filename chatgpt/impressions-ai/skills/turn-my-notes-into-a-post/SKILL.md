# Turn my notes into a post

Paste your notes, a voice-memo transcript or something a client said. We'll find the post in it.

## How to use it

Call the Impressions AI tool `get_skill` with `name: "turn-my-notes-into-a-post"`, and as `inputs` whatever the member has already told you. Follow what it returns for the whole conversation. Never do this skill from memory: its steps live on the Impressions AI server, kept current by the Impressions team.

If the Impressions AI tools aren't there, follow "If the tools aren't there" in the impressions-ai skill.

## How members ask for it

- **From a transcript:** This is a transcript of me talking through an idea: [paste transcript]. Find the post in it, and ask me anything you need.
- **From scattered notes:** Here are my scattered notes: [notes]. Which post type do they fit best? Help me shape them into a draft.
- **From a conversation:** A client said this to me on a call: "[their words]." Help me turn it into a post using the Impressions method.
