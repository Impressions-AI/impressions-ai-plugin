# Write a personal story

Turn a moment from your life or work into a post your ideal client sees themselves in.

Post type: Personal Story (`personal-story`).

## How to use it

Call the Impressions AI tool `get_skill` with `name: "write-a-personal-story"`, and as `inputs` whatever the member has already told you. Follow what it returns for the whole conversation. Never do this skill from memory: its steps live on the Impressions AI server, kept current by the Impressions team.

If the Impressions AI tools aren't there, follow "If the tools aren't there" in the impressions-ai skill.

## How members ask for it

- **A moment that taught you something:** Help me turn this moment from my career into a personal story post: [what happened]. Ask me questions to find the lesson.
- **A mistake you made:** I made this mistake early on: [the mistake]. Help me share it in a personal story post that helps my ideal client.
