# What should I post?

Not sure what to post? A few questions, then a handful of ideas that are yours.

## How to use it

Call the Impressions AI tool `get_skill` with `name: "what-should-i-post"`, and as `inputs` whatever the member has already told you. Follow what it returns for the whole conversation. Never do this skill from memory: its steps live on the Impressions AI server, kept current by the Impressions team.

If the Impressions AI tools aren't there, follow "If the tools aren't there" in the impressions-ai skill.

## How members ask for it

- **From scratch:** I need post ideas. I'm a [what you do] and I help [who]. Ask me a few questions first.
- **For one post type:** Give me three personal story ideas from my work this month. Ask me what's been happening first.
