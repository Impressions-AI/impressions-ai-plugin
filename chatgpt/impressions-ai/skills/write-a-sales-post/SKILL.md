# Write a sales post

Write about your offer or a client result without sounding pushy.

Post type: Sales Content (`sales-content`).

## How to use it

Call the Impressions AI tool `get_skill` with `name: "write-a-sales-post"`, and as `inputs` whatever the member has already told you. Follow what it returns for the whole conversation. Never do this skill from memory: its steps live on the Impressions AI server, kept current by the Impressions team.

If the Impressions AI tools aren't there, follow "If the tools aren't there" in the impressions-ai skill.

## How members ask for it

- **From a result:** Help me turn a client win into a sales content post. The result: [what changed]. Keep the client anonymous.
- **Introduce your offer:** Help me write a sales content post about [my offer] for [my ideal client]. I don't want it to sound pushy.
- **Answer a doubt:** People often hesitate to work with me because [the objection]. Help me write a post that answers it honestly.
