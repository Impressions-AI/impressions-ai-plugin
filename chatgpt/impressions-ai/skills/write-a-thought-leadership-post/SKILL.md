# Write a thought leadership post

Share what you believe about your field, and why, so the right people trust your thinking.

Post type: Thought Leadership (`thought-leadership`).

## How to use it

Call the Impressions AI tool `get_skill` with `name: "write-a-thought-leadership-post"`, and as `inputs` whatever the member has already told you. Follow what it returns for the whole conversation. Never do this skill from memory: its steps live on the Impressions AI server, kept current by the Impressions team.

If the Impressions AI tools aren't there, follow "If the tools aren't there" in the impressions-ai skill.

## How members ask for it

- **A belief you'd defend:** Most people in my field say [common advice]. I think [my view]. Help me turn that into a thought leadership post.
- **A question you keep hearing:** My clients keep asking me [the question]. Help me answer it in a thought leadership post.
