# Plan my week

One post of each type for the week, drawn from what's actually happening in your work.

## How to use it

Call the Impressions AI tool `get_skill` with `name: "plan-my-week"`, and as `inputs` whatever the member has already told you. Follow what it returns for the whole conversation. Never do this skill from memory: its steps live on the Impressions AI server, kept current by the Impressions team.

If the Impressions AI tools aren't there, follow "If the tools aren't there" in the impressions-ai skill.

## How members ask for it

- **One of each type:** Help me plan a week of LinkedIn posts: one thought leadership, one personal story and one sales content. My work is [what I do] and my ideal client is [who]. Suggest an idea for each.
- **From what's on your mind:** Here's what's been happening in my work lately: [a few lines]. Which three post ideas are worth writing this week, and which type is each?
