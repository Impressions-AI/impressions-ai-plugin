# Is my post ready?

See how your draft rates on the Post-Format Checklist, and what to change.

## How to use it

Call the Impressions AI tool `get_skill` with `name: "is-my-post-ready"`, and as `inputs` whatever the member has already told you. Follow what it returns for the whole conversation. Never do this skill from memory: its steps live on the Impressions AI server, kept current by the Impressions team.

If the Impressions AI tools aren't there, follow "If the tools aren't there" in the impressions-ai skill.

## How members ask for it

- **A quick check:** Check this [thought leadership / personal story / sales content] draft against the Post-Format Checklist and tell me what's working and what's missing: [paste draft].
- **Check, then fix:** Check my draft against the Post-Format Checklist. Then suggest the smallest changes that would make the biggest difference: [paste draft].
